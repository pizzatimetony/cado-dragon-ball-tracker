System reference

Rules, kept simple.

These are starting guidelines, not a menu limiting what Cado can attempt. The GM makes rulings for unusual actions.

Core principle. You choose what Cado does. Rolls settle genuinely uncertain outcomes; stronger opponents remain dangerous. No fudging, no automatic wins, and no scripted immunity from consequences.
Checks and combat basics

d20 + attribute + situational modifier against a difficulty or opponent's Defense. Typical difficulties: moderate 10, challenging 13, difficult 16, extremely difficult 20, exceptional 25. Defense starts at 10 + Speed; initiative is d20 + Speed. A round allows an action, movement, and a reaction.

Natural 20 on an attack doubles damage dice. Distance uses Close, Near, and Far zones; typical movement changes one zone.

Power ranks

Equal ranks: tactics and rolls matter most. A one-rank advantage gives the stronger fighter +2 to attacks and Defense, +2 damage, and 1 damage reduction. A two-rank advantage makes those +4, +4, and 2 respectively. At a gap of three or more ranks, ordinary direct attacks generally cannot meaningfully injure the stronger fighter without a credible weakness or exceptional circumstance.

Power ranks are relative and are never applied twice. A lower-powered character can still escape, outthink, delay, or accomplish a goal without winning a direct fight.

Defeat and recovery

At 0 HP, Cado is incapacitated. On subsequent turns roll a d20 vs. 10: three successes stabilize, three failures mean death. A natural 1 counts as two failures; a natural 20 restores 1 HP. Additional damage can accelerate death. Injuries can persist. Normal rest restores Ki and Stamina and heals only 1d6 + Durability HP without treatment. The enemy's motives, not plot protection, determine whether defeat leads to capture, mercy, abandonment, or death.

Progression and training

Growth follows meaningful accomplishments and training. An advancement may raise an attribute, add 4 max HP, add 2 max Ki or Stamina, or develop a technique. Raising Durability also adds 4 max HP. Power Rank increases are larger, rarer breakthroughs. Training takes in-world time. Transformations require established prerequisites. Saiyan recovery is not an automatic exploit.

World and campaign continuity

The story starts around Age 749 during original Dragon Ball. Cado is a Universe 6 Saiyan born away from Sadala, while Goku's canon begins in Universe 7. Cross-universe travel isn't ordinary spaceflight. The opening campaign runs in parallel, with original characters and locations; canon characters and events continue independently unless changed through credible events. Planet Varka and age 18 are proposals, not established canon or final decisions.

Randomness policy

The webpage rolls with the browser's crypto.getRandomValues, using rejection sampling to avoid modulo bias. It won't roll at all if cryptographic randomness is unavailable. The GM will use Python's secrets.randbelow for dice rolled inside ChatGPT. These are cryptographically secure, unpredictable random draws, rather than a physical source of provable “true” randomness. A log of browser rolls is kept on the Dice tab. Webpage rolls are local and do not automatically sync to chat.
